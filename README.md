### 🔬 Featured Research

#### 📄 *The interpreter’s trap: how explainable AI launders uncertainty into justification—a socio-legal case study of COMPAS risk assessment*

> **Abstract:** This paper explores the socio-legal implications of Explainable AI (XAI) within the judicial system, specifically focusing on how XAI tools can inadvertently mask uncertainty and provide a false sense of justification in risk assessment algorithms like COMPAS.

- **Journal**: AI & Society (2026)
